Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

We strive to process all orders within 1–2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once your order has been dispatched.

  • UK Standard: £3.99 (2–4 Business Days)
  • UK Express: £6.99 (Next Business Day)
  • International: Calculated at checkout (7–14 Business Days)

Free shipping on all UK orders over £75.

Return & Refund Policy

We want you to love your Clarity gear. If something isn't right, you have 30 days from the delivery date to return it to us.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Unworn and unwashed
  • In the same condition that you received it
  • In the original packaging with tags attached

How to Return

To initiate a return, please email us at returns@clarity.com with your Order ID. We will provide you with a return authorization slip and the nearest warehouse address.

Please note that customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or incorrect.
