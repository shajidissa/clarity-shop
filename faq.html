Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

For UK orders, standard delivery takes 2-4 business days. International orders typically arrive within 7-14 days depending on the destination.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging. See our Shipping & Returns page for more details.

How do your sizes run?

Our hoodies are a "Relaxed Fit" by default. If you prefer a tighter fit, we recommend sizing down. If you want an oversized look, stick to your usual size or go one up.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship globally! Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your location.

How do I wash my Clarity hoodie?

To keep your hoodie soft and the print fresh, wash inside out on a cold cycle (30°C) and hang to dry. Avoid tumble drying.
